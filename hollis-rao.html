Services

We take a project from the first site visit to handover, or join at any stage.

Architecture

Concept design, approvals drawings, construction drawings and site supervision for new buildings and extensions.

Interiors

Layouts, joinery, lighting and material schedules, designed together with the building rather than after it.

Feasibility studies

Site analysis, area statements and a cost range before you commit to a plot or a brief.

How a project runs

Four stages, each ending in a decision from you before we move on.

Stage 1

Brief and site

We visit the site, listen to how you will use the building and agree the budget and programme.

Stage 2

Concept

Plans, sections and a physical model, usually two options, so the decision is easy to see.

Stage 3

Detailed design

Approval drawings, structure, services and a bill of quantities for builders to price.

Stage 4

Construction

Fortnightly site visits, checks against the drawings and a snag list at handover.
